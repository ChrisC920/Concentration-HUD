FocusMate.

A wearable-driven focus monitor that fuses your first-person POV with your computer screen to detect when you've drifted — and gently pulls you back.

Ray-Ban Meta WebRTC macOS · YOLOv8n Gemini 3 Flash Raspberry Pi Turret

Christopher Cho · 2026-05-06

01 · Problem why focus is broken

Knowing you're distracted is the easy part. Catching it in time isn't.

01

Self-report fails

Pomodoro timers and to-do apps trust you to be honest with yourself. You aren't.

02

Screen alone is blind

Time-tracking apps see your active window — not whether you're staring at the ceiling.

03

Eye-tracking is gated

Real eye-tracking hardware is expensive, calibration-heavy, and lab-bound.

02 · Insight the wedge

Smart glasses already see what you see.

If we capture the first-person POV and compare it to what's on your laptop screen in real time, we can ask two questions a screen-only tool can't:

Q1

Are your eyes on the laptop?

If your POV doesn't match the screen, you're looking somewhere else — phone, snack, daydream.

Q2

Is the screen on-task?

An LLM checks the screen content against your stated goal. YouTube while writing a paper? Off-task.

04 · Data Flow two streams converge

How the signals meet on the Mac

Stream A · 6 fps

POV camera frames

Glasses → iOS → WebRTC → Mac → GazeAnalyzer

Decoded as RTCVideoFrame, processed at ~6 fps off the render thread. Streaming pipeline is untouched.

Stream B · every 2s

Display capture

ScreenCaptureKit → ScreenHashStore + RelevanceChecker

Two outputs: a compact reference fingerprint for the gaze matcher, and a downscaled JPEG sent to Gemini every 15s.

05 · Gaze Pipeline the hard part

"Is the user looking at a focus-work object?"

Per camera frame, ~3 fps:

01

YOLOv8n

CoreML detector over the POV frame. Fast, lightweight, on-device.

02

Class filter

Keep only focus classes: laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, book.

03

Confidence ≥ 0.22

Tuned low — laptops at oblique/close angles often score 0.25–0.35.

04

Center-ROI overlap

Bbox must put ≥12% of its area in the 60% center box: "the user is looking at it."

05

Asymmetric vote

2-of-5 to flip ON, 5-of-5 to flip OFF. Glances don't trigger; sustained absence does.

window=5 · ON=2 · OFF=5  |  ~0.6s to engage, ~1.5s to disengage

06 · Relevance Pipeline the LLM judge

"Is what's on screen actually about my goal?"

Distraction-aware prompt

  • Explicit OFF-TASK list: YouTube, TikTok, Twitter/X, Reddit, Netflix, news feeds, games.
  • Anti-rationalization rule: a YouTube tutorial titled "How to learn X" is OFF-TASK — passive watching ≠ doing.
  • Explicit ON-TASK list: IDE, terminal, docs, GitHub, Figma, notes about the goal.
// Strict-JSON prompt to Gemini 3 Flash goal: "writing CMSC730 paper" image: <screenshot.jpg> reply: { "surface": "YouTube watch page", "on_task": false, "reason": "video feed unrelated to writing" }
Robustness

4-attempt exponential backoff on 429/500/503; transient errors don't pollute the UI.

Cost

1 call / 15s = ~240 calls/hour. Flash pricing keeps a session under a few cents.

Privacy

API key stays in UserDefaults; no screenshot leaves the device unless monitor is running.

07 · Decision FocusCoordinator

Four states. One rule: gaze beats screen.

.onTask

Eyes on screen + screen relevant

Menu bar dot is green. App stays out of the way.

.lookingAway

POV ≠ screen for > N seconds

Configurable timeout (default 3s for demo). Notification fires once on transition.

.offTopic(reason)

Screen content fails goal check

Gemini's reason text is shown. User decides whether to refocus or update goal.

.unknown

Insufficient evidence

Cold start, missing API key, or fingerprint loop hasn't filled. Stays neutral.

08 · Intervention the turret loop

When focus breaks, the turret shoots back.

A Raspberry Pi sits on the desk with a camera. On every transition out of .onTask, the Mac fires a one-line HTTP trigger; a few seconds later a clip lands in the app.

01

Trigger

POST http://turret.local:8787/shoot on focus loss.

02

Pi records

Few-second MP4 (H.264) of the user. Hosted at /last_clip.mp4.

03

Mac pulls

Polls HEAD until Content-Length stabilizes — file is fully written.

04

Show

AVKit player appears in-app only after the clip is fully on disk.

Pull, not push

No inbound listener on the Mac, no Local Network entitlement, no firewall prompt.

Stable-size gate

Two consecutive HEADs with the same length → file done. Avoids partial reads.

Single source

Both real focus loss and a manual Test /shoot button drive the same fetch path.

11 · Hard Parts honest tradeoffs

Things that didn't work — and why we changed them

v1 — heuristic rectangles

Edge detection + luminance gates

Rejected smooth walls but mistook picture frames and doors for screens. Tunable but brittle.

v2 — content matching

NCC + Vision featureprint vs. live display

Worked when the screen filled the frame. Failed at typing distance and on bright hallways (smooth → unstable similarity).

v3 — current

YOLOv8n + center-ROI + asymmetric vote

One CoreML detector replaces the entire stack. Knows what a laptop is, not just what a screen looks like. Vote bias makes it forgiving without being naive.

screen judge

Strict → forgiving → distraction-aware

v1 prompt was too strict (flagged research as off-task). v2 was too lenient (let YouTube pass). v3 names distraction surfaces explicitly.

12 · Limitations where it breaks

Honest limits of v1

L1

POV coverage

If the camera FOV barely sees the screen, similarity is weak regardless of pipeline.

L2

Multi-monitor users

Currently fingerprints only the primary display. Looking at a second monitor reads as "looking away."

L3

LLM cost & latency

15s polling is a tradeoff. Lower → costs grow + 503s. Higher → user can drift unseen.

L4

Privacy surface

Screen pixels leave the device when checking relevance. Documented; never default-on.
